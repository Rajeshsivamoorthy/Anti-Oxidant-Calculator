Formulas & notes
  • Standard curve: Abs = m·Conc + c by least squares (R² shown). Test-solution Conc = (Abs − blank − c) / m.
  • Content per g extract = Conc × DF × V(mL) / W(mg). (µg/mL × mL / mg = mg/g; µM × mL / mg = µmol/g.)
  • % scavenging = (Acontrol − (Asample − colour blank)) / Acontrol × 100. IC50 = (50 − c)/m from linear regression of mean % vs concentration — use concentrations in the linear range that bracket 50 %.
  • Relative potency = IC50 standard / IC50 sample (>1 means sample is stronger than the standard).
  • SD is the sample standard deviation (n − 1). Typical wavelengths: TPC 765 nm, TFC 415 nm, tannins 700 nm, FRAP 593 nm, DPPH 517 nm, ABTS 734 nm, H2O2 230 nm.
  • The OH and H2O2 formulas assume absorbance falls as scavenging rises; adapt to your protocol if it differs. Example values are placeholders, not real data.
